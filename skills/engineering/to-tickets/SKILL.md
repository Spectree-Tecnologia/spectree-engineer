The squad no longer breaks work into tickets. If you are reading this, you belong to a session that started before Goal Driven Development and reached the step that used to call `to-tickets`.

Nothing is lost and nothing is redone. Do not write tickets.

1. The Skill tool printed `Base directory for this skill: ...`. The plugin's root is three folders above it. Read `commands/engineer.md` there, section 3, "Projeto em andamento de antes do Goal Driven".
2. If you are the orchestrator (you talk to the user and dispatch the others): follow that section. The item you were building continues as a goal, from the stage it is in, on the same branch. Its contract comes from the `to-goal` skill, which knows how to turn a spec and tickets into one.
3. If you were dispatched with a mission: carry on with it. A ticket counts as your contract, one success criterion per acceptance criterion; your card says the rest.
