# Glossary

- **Condominium**: the tenant. Every row that belongs to a customer carries its condominium.
- **Unit**: one apartment or house of a Condominium.
- **Resident**: a person who lives in a Unit and signs in to see what belongs to it.
- **Charge**: an amount a Unit owes, issued by billing, with a status and a stored PDF.
- **Second copy**: the PDF of a Charge sent again to a Resident of its Unit.
- **Outbox**: the record the notifications module keeps of every message before it is sent.
